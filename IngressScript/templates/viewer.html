Split this voice

Name the people mixed into this voice, then sort lines with the keys 1–4. Each line plays by itself. S skips (it stays as it is), Z goes back, Space replays, and Enter accepts the suggestion. After a few lines per person, “Suggest the rest” sorts lines by voice.
